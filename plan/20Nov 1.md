### Architectural Design: V3.1 Multi-Strike Auto-Rotation + Production Upgrades

#### 1. Multi-Strike Watchlist (`CANDIDATE_STRIKES`)

* `config.py` (ya startup prompt) mein tum ek ordered list doge — closest ATM/OTM se lekar deeper OTM tak (`3 to 5 strikes`), for example:
```python
CANDIDATE_STRIKES = [
    "SENSEX26NOV2684100CE",
    "SENSEX26NOV2684300CE",
    "SENSEX26NOV2684500CE",
    "SENSEX26NOV2684700CE",
]
PREMIUM_MAX_THRESHOLD = 330.0  # Agar active strike ₹330 cross kare -> agle OTM strike par shift!
PREMIUM_MIN_THRESHOLD = 140.0  # Agar active strike bahut decay/drop ho -> pichhle closer strike par shift!

```

- **Smart State Tracking (Important Quant Detail!):**
  - Sirf active strike ke candles record karne ki jagah, engine **har tick cycle mein saari 4 strikes ke quotes (`ltp`, `bid`, `ask`)** fetch karega (4 lightweight calls = ~0.6s total) aur **har strike ka apna `candles`, `Base High`, aur `Base Low` parallel mein maintain karega**!
  - **Iska sabse bada fayda:** Jab `84100CE` ₹330 cross karega aur engine `84300CE` par switch karega, toh `84300CE` ke paas **10:30–11:30 AM ka apna poora horizontal `Base High` aur `Base Low` pehle se ready** hoga — zero observation delay!

#### 2. Rotation Trigger Rules (Kab Strike Switch Hogi?)
- **Rule A (Only When Flat / Not in Trade):** Jab koi trade active nahi hai (`not in_trade`), aur active strike ka `ltp > PREMIUM_MAX_THRESHOLD` (e.g., `₹330`) ho jaaye:
  - Engine automatically list ke **next further OTM strike** par shift ho jayega jiska `ltp <= PREMIUM_MAX_THRESHOLD` aur `bid-ask spread <= ₹1.00` ho.
- **Rule B (Reverse Pullback Rotation):** Agar market pullback kare aur active OTM strike `₹140` ke neeche gir jaaye, toh engine wapas list ke **previous higher-delta strike** par shift ho sakta hai (jab tak uska `ltp <= ₹330` ho).
- **Rule C (Locked During Active Trade):** Ek baar trade entry ho jaane ke baad strike **100% locked** rahegi jab tak exit na ho jaaye.

#### 3. Complete V3.1 Upgrade Bundle (4 Improvements in One Clean File)
1. **Multi-Strike Parallel Base Tracking + Auto OTM Rotation (`PREMIUM_MAX_THRESHOLD = 330.0`)**.
2. **Multi-Trade Loop (`3/3` Daily Cap):** Ek trade close hone ke baad script exit nahi hogi — automatically reset hokar agle valid breakout ke liye scan continue karegi jab tak `Trades < 3` aur `Daily Net P&L > -₹800` aur `Time <= 1:30 PM`.
3. **Adaptive Stage 1 Breakeven (`1:1.5` R:R):** Fixed `+25%` ki jagah **`entry_price + 1.5 * (entry_price - initial_sl)`** par SL Cost-to-Cost lock hoga (har strike price par consistent math!).
4. **Live Bid-Ask Spread Gate:** Order bhejne se pehle `ask - bid <= MAX_SPREAD` verify karega — wide/illiquid spikes par slip nahi hoga.
