### Architect Solution: Structural Base SL + Max Rupeez/Points Cap Hybrid

Trending days par agar 60-minute ya 4-candle base thoda wide ho (`35–60 pts`), toh poora `Base Low` SL rakhne ki jagah ek **Dual-Cap Stop-Loss** use karna quant standard hota hai:

* **Effective SL = `max(Base Low, Entry - MAX_SL_POINTS)**` (where `MAX_SL_POINTS = 25.0` pts = **₹500** max risk on 20 qty, ya `30.0` pts = **₹600** max risk).
* **Isse kya hoga?**
1. Agar base tight hai (say `18 pts`), toh natural `Base Low` hi SL rahega.
2. Agar base wide/trending hai (jaise second trade mein `39.25 pts` tha), toh engine trade ko cancel karne ki jagah SL ko **Entry - 25 pts (`₹500` risk)** (ya breakout candle ke low) par cap karke **trade le lega**, aur phir Stage 1 (`+25%` BE) aur Stage 2 (`+50%` 2-candle trail) se ride karega!
3. Saath mein ek **Chasing Guard** rakhenge: agar breakout candle ka close `Base High` se **> 5% upar** jump kar chuka hai (jaise pehle run mein `352.25` se seedha `367.30` par close hua), toh hi skip karega taaki top-tick par chase na ho.
