### Quant Mentor Note for Today's 15–20 Trade Audit

When reviewing your CSV logs after today's session, pay special attention to **Stage 1 (`+25%` gain breakeven)** vs **`1:1` R:R breakeven**:

* On cheap OTM/ATM options (say ₹80 premium), `+25%` is `+20 pts`, which might be *wider* than a `12 pt` base risk (`1:1`), leaving you exposed longer.
* On expensive ITM/SENSEX options (say ₹350 premium), `+25%` is `+87.5 pts`—huge! If your initial base risk was only `25 pts`, waiting for `+87.5 pts` (`3.5R`!) before moving SL to cost means you'll take avoidable full `-1R` losses on trades that rallied `+2R` and reversed.
* **Actionable idea for your CSV logger:** Log `initial_risk_pts`, `max_favorable_excursion_pts` (`MFE`), and `max_adverse_excursion_pts` (`MAE`) on every trade. After 15 trades, `MFE` distribution will mathematically tell you the exact optimal breakeven trigger!
