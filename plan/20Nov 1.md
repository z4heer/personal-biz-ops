### 3. New Strategy Blueprint: Intraday Cash Equity (`NSE`) Buy & Sell Engine

Tumhara idea bahut strong hai — **Intraday Cash Equity (`NSE`)** mein **no theta decay, no gamma spike, aur 5x intraday margin** milta hai, aur tumhara same core framework (**10:30–11:30 Base + VWAP + Volume Surge + Market Structure**) wahan aur bhi zyada predictable kaam karta hai!

Iske liye hum ek dedicated **`equity_intraday_engine.py`** banayenge (same `broker_adapter.py` aur `OpenAlgo` pipeline reuse karke). Iska exact architecture kuch aisa hoga:

#### A. Pre-Market Stock Universe Filter (09:15 – 10:30 AM)

* **Liquidity:** Nifty 50 / Nifty Next 50 stocks (`Price: ₹300 – ₹3,000`, tight ₹0.05–₹0.10 spread).
* **Relative Volume (RVOL):** Opening 1-hour volume `>= 1.5x` of 10-day average volume (institutional footprint).
* **Clean Structure:** Stock clearly above VWAP (Long bias) OR clearly below VWAP (Short bias) — **no choppy mid-VWAP stocks**.

#### B. Automated Entry State Machine (`11:30 AM – 01:30 PM IST`)

1. **10:30 – 11:30 AM Consolidation Box (`Base High` & `Base Low`):**
* Stock ka consolidation box width `<= 1.2%` of stock price hona chahiye (tight coil = explosive breakout).


2. **Dual-Side Trigger (`BUY` & `SHORT SELL`):**
* **Long Setup (`BUY`):**
* 3m candle closes `> Base High`
* `Close > Session VWAP` AND `9 EMA > 21 EMA`
* Breakout candle volume `>= 1.5x` average of last 5 candles
* **Hard SL:** `max(Base Low, Entry - 0.6% of Entry)`


* **Short Setup (`SELL`):**
* 3m candle closes `< Base Low`
* `Close < Session VWAP` AND `9 EMA < 21 EMA`
* Breakdown candle volume `>= 1.5x` average of last 5 candles
* **Hard SL:** `min(Base High, Entry + 0.6% of Entry)`





#### C. Position Sizing & Trailing Exit (Risk-Normalized)

* **Dynamic Quantity Formula:**

$$\text{Qty} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Max Risk per Trade (₹400)}}{\vert{}\text{Entry} - \text{SL}\vert{}} \right\rfloor$$



*(Example: ₹1,000 stock par SL ₹6 door hai -> `400 / 6 = 66 shares` — har trade par exact ₹400 fixed risk!)*
* **Trailing Protocol:**
* **`1:1.5` R:R:** Move SL to **Cost-to-Cost (Breakeven)**
* **`1:2.5` R:R:** Trail SL using **Last 2-Candle Swing Low/High** OR **9 EMA close break**
* **Hard Target:** `1:3.5` R:R or **03:15 PM IST** auto square-off (MIS intraday square-off).
